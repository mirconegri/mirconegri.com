01 Strata

Who I am

A core sample, not a summary: what I studied, what I build, and what I do when neither of those is happening.

I study computer science at the University of Trento because I want to know how things work, not only how to use them.

That usually turns into software nobody asked for and somebody needed: a Telegram bot that replaced the laundry booking sheet in my residence, a planner that turns a scout camp into a menu and a shopping list, a pipeline that checks machined parts on a conveyor.

Besides university I teach computer science in digital inclusion programmes. Explaining a machine to someone who has never trusted one is a different craft from programming it, and I think it made me a better developer.

The rest of my time goes to voluntary service — first aid, food distribution, and fourteen years of scouting — plus music and whatever I decided to learn this month.
